Key insight: The classical FP (⟨k_friend⟩ ≥ ⟨k⟩ and ⟨k_nn⟩ ≥ ⟨k⟩) always holds, but says nothing about how many nodes are individually dominated. φ_global and φ_local can both fall below ½ even while the FP inequalities hold — and they can diverge from each other when neighbor-degree distributions are skewed.
